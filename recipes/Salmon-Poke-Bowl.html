Salmon Poke Bowl

A salmon poke bowl is a refreshing and healthy dish that combines fresh salmon with a variety of vegetables and seasonings. It's a popular choice for those looking for a lighter alternative to traditional sushi rolls.

Ingredients

  • Salmon fillet
  • White rice
  • Avocado
  • Cucumber
  • Carrot
  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Sesame seeds
