Spaghetti Bolognese

Spaghetti Bolognese is a classic Italian pasta dish that features a rich and savory meat sauce. It's a favorite comfort food for many and is often served with grated Parmesan cheese on top.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Ground beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Italian herbs (basil, oregano)
