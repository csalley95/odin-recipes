California Roll

California roll is an inside-out sushi roll (uramaki) where the sushi rice is on the outside and the fillings and nori are rolled inside. As the name might suggest, the California roll did not originate in Japan. Rather, Japanese-born sushi chef Hidekazu Tojo of Vancouver, Canada claims to have invented it at his restaurant in the 1960s after learning that Westerners were apprehensive about trying raw fish. Today, California rolls are synonymous with sushi in the West.

Ingredients

  • Nori (seaweed) sheets
  • Toasted white sesame seeds
  • Japanese short-grain white rice
  • Water
  • Kombu (dried kelp)
  • Seasoned rice vinegar (sushi vinegar)
  • Crabmeat
  • Japanese Kewpie mayonnaise
  • Avocado
